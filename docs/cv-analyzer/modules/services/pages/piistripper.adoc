= PII Stripper Service

The piistripper consumes durable redaction jobs from `QUEUE_NAME.pii` and reads the extracted text from the shared S3-compatible bucket. After successful redaction it writes `results/<job-id>.redacted.txt`; it does not overwrite or delete `results/<job-id>.txt`.

== Detection and output

The service runs without external model/API calls. It applies local patterns for email addresses, URLs, phone numbers, and common street-address forms, plus the local English named-entity recognizer in `github.com/jdkato/prose/v2` for person and organization names. It replaces matches with typed placeholders such as `[EMAIL]`, `[ADDRESS]`, `[PERSON]`, and `[ORGANIZATION]`.

This is best-effort redaction, not guaranteed anonymization. The English-only entity recognizer and patterns can miss direct, contextual, or indirect identifiers and can produce false positives. The separate original text remains personal data and is still subject to the applicable access-control and retention/deletion policy. Do not describe this service alone as GDPR compliance.

== Delivery and failure behavior

The extractor publishes the redaction job only after storing the extracted result and acknowledges its original queue delivery only after RabbitMQ confirms the redaction publish. Piistripper validates that the job ID is a UUID, the bucket matches its configured bucket, and the object key is exactly `results/<job-id>.txt`.

The worker uses manual acknowledgements, a prefetch of one, deterministic output keys, a 10 MiB source-result limit, and up to three retries after the initial attempt. It republishes exhausted or malformed jobs to `QUEUE_NAME.pii.failed`. Terminal progress is published to `QUEUE_NAME.events`; the user-facing job becomes `completed` only after the redacted output is stored. Delivery is at-least-once, so duplicate work may occur.

== Configuration

Piistripper shares `RABBITMQ_URL`, `QUEUE_NAME`, and all `RUSTFS_*` settings with the uploader and extractor. It reads objects from `RUSTFS_BUCKET`; deployment credentials should be limited to reading the extracted result and writing the redacted result. See xref:configuration.adoc[configuration] and xref:operations.adoc[operations].
