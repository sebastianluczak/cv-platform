= Architecture Drivers

This project was born with the goal of:

. Learning and experimenting with new technologies, tools and frameworks, Go in particular.
. Self promotion and personal branding on GitHub and LinkedIn via building a portfolio of projects to showcase skills and experience.
. Cementing a personal knowledge base and reference library of software engineering concepts, patterns, practices and principles.

== Outside-in and Inside-out

Due to the nature of this project, the architecture drivers are both outside-in and inside-out.

=== Outside-in

The outside-in drivers are the external forces that shape the architecture of the system.

=== Inside-out

The inside-out drivers are the internal forces that shape the architecture of the system.

=== Leveraging drivers

This project uses:

. ADRs to capture and communicate architecture decisions.
. Architecture drivers to capture and communicate the forces that shape the architecture of the system.
. Incremental and iterative development to evolve the architecture of the system over time.

This project is not:

. A production system, but a learning and experimentation platform.
. Using third-party services or APIs to achieve its goals, but rather building its own services and components from scratch.
. A monolithic system, but a microservices-based system that is composed of small, independent services that communicate with each other via asynchronous messaging.
