ADR-0005: Usage of AI in cv-analyzer

Context

Nowadays, AI in IT is everywhere and we cannot escape it.

We can accept it and go with the flow, learning new things or stay behind and hope to stay afloat.

Decision

This project heavily uses agentic workflows, tools, prompts, instructions and every other possible method of AI-influence to deliver code:

  1. faster

  2. without bugs

  3. with more checks and tests

  4. without exploding my family budget
